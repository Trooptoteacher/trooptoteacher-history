1.1 Purpose of This Dossier

This package is a Waiver-Readiness and State Review Evidence Dossier prepared by TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC for U.S. History: Reconstruction to Present, a comprehensive instructional materials package for Tennessee public school grades 9–12.

The dossier is designed to serve a single purpose: to demonstrate that TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC has already assembled the documentation, alignment evidence, and instructional infrastructure that a Tennessee Local Education Agency (LEA) or state reviewer would typically need to evaluate whether these materials are complete enough to support a waiver request, supplemental adoption, or formal state review pathway.

This dossier is not a district sales document. It is not a claim of state approval. It is not tied to any specific LEA or adoption cycle. It is a compliance-oriented evidence package organized to reduce reviewer workload and demonstrate documentation completeness.

What This Dossier Is A structured collection of documentation demonstrating: (1) standards alignment completeness; (2) instructional design quality consistent with Tennessee rubric criteria; (3) statutory compliance with applicable Tennessee Code provisions; (4) materials completeness across all required components; and (5) accessibility provisions for students with disabilities and English language learners.
What This Dossier Is Not This dossier does not constitute state approval of TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC materials by the Tennessee Textbook and Instructional Materials Quality Commission, the State Board of Education, or the Tennessee Department of Education. No waiver is guaranteed by the existence or submission of this package. LEAs must follow their own legal counsel's guidance and the applicable waiver process under State Board Rule 0520-01-18.

1.2 Legal Pathways This Package Supports

Tennessee law provides multiple pathways under which TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC materials may be used in public school classrooms:

Path A: Supplemental Use Available Now

Authority: TCA § 49-6-2202(a)(3)

Tennessee law expressly exempts supplemental instructional materials from the state-approved adoption list requirement. TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC materials may be used as supplemental materials alongside a district's primary adopted textbook without Textbook Commission approval or State Board waiver.

Limitation: Materials classified as supplemental may not serve as the primary 180-day instructional program for a course.

Path B: Primary Use via Waiver Requires LEA Action

Authority: TCA § 49-6-2206; State Board Rule 0520-01-18

An LEA wishing to use TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC materials as its primary instructional program for U.S. History (in place of a commission-approved text) must apply to the State Board of Education for a waiver. This dossier is designed to reduce the documentation burden of that waiver application.

Key requirement: Waiver applications must include a completed Schedule F rubric and standards alignment table.

Path C: Commission Submission Future Cycle

Authority: TCA § 49-6-2201; TCA § 49-6-2202

TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC intends to submit materials for formal Textbook Commission review during the appropriate Social Studies adoption cycle. This dossier documents the preparation completed toward that submission. Contact TDOE at [email protected] for current cycle timing.

Path D: HQIM Designation In Progress

Authority: TCA § 49-6-2207(e); TDOE HQIM Initiative

Districts completing a supplemental HQIM adoption must submit ED-2153 (board-signed) and ED-5099 (adoption list) to TDOE pursuant to T.C.A. § 49-6-2207(e). Deadline for Schedule E cycle: June 30, 2026. TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC has prepared a pre-filled ED-5099 product information sheet for district use.

3.1 TCA § 49-6-2202 — The Critical Supplemental Exemption

TCA § 49-6-2202(a)(3)
"Except as provided in § 49-6-2206(b), this part does not prohibit the use of, or apply to, supplemental instructional materials."
✅ How this applies to TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC: When positioned as supplemental materials alongside a district's primary adopted textbook, TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC materials do not require Textbook Commission approval or a State Board waiver. This is the legally available pathway for immediate district use. The single restriction that applies even to supplementals is the Common Core prohibition in § 49-6-2206(b) — TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC is aligned to Tennessee Academic Standards, not Common Core.
TCA § 49-6-2202(c)(4)
Diversity and Tennessee History Requirement
"In recommending textbooks and instructional materials for use in social studies, Tennessee history, American history or any related subject, the commission shall strive to recommend textbooks and instructional materials that accurately and comprehensively portray the full range of diversity and achievement of racial and ethnic minorities as well as the role and importance of religion in history."
✅ TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC compliance: Racial and ethnic minorities represented across all 10 units; religion's historical role (Puritanism, Social Gospel, Scopes Trial, Civil Rights Movement) addressed; Tennessee's diverse history — including African American political leaders, the Highlander Folk School, and the Nashville Sit-ins — prominently featured.

3.3 TCA § 49-6-2206 — Restrictions That Apply Even to Supplementals

TCA § 49-6-2206(b)(2)(A)
Common Core Prohibition — Applies to All Materials Including Supplementals
"A teacher or principal in a public school of this state shall not use or permit to be used in the person's school, whether as a supplement to the LEA's or school's adopted textbooks and instructional materials or otherwise, textbooks or instructional materials created to align exclusively with the Common Core State Standards..."
✅ TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC is aligned to Tennessee Academic Standards (current 2016 standards and 2024 revised standards per crosswalk). These are Tennessee standards, not Common Core. No Common Core alignment is claimed, marketed, or present in these materials. Standards documentation confirms Tennessee alignment throughout.
TCA § 49-6-2206(b)(1)
Fund Withholding for Violations
"If the commissioner of education finds that an LEA knowingly violated this section, then the commissioner shall withhold state funds, in an amount determined by the commissioner, from the LEA until the LEA is in compliance."
✅ TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC mitigates this risk for LEAs by: (1) confirming Tennessee standards alignment in every unit file; (2) providing a signed vendor attestation of compliance; and (3) documenting non-Common-Core status in the Standards Alignment Matrix. LEAs that use TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC materials as supplementals consistent with TCA § 49-6-2202(a)(3) face no fund withholding risk under this provision.

3.5 TCA § 49-6-1019 — Prohibited Concepts

TCA § 49-6-1019
Prohibited Concepts in Instruction — Applies to Supplemental Materials
Prohibits instruction or supplemental materials that include or promote: (1) one race or sex is inherently superior; (2) an individual of one race bears responsibility for actions committed by others of the same race; (3) an individual should feel discomfort, guilt, or anguish based on race or sex; (4) moral character is determined by race or sex; and related concepts.
⚠ TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC approach: Historical content in Units 1 (Reconstruction), 6 (WWII), and 7 (Civil Rights) requires careful compliance review under this statute. TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC's design philosophy — factual historical narrative, SOAPS primary source analysis, multiple perspectives, no advocacy or collective guilt framing — is intended to comply. However, a VLAA attorney review of these specific units is recommended before commercial distribution. Contact: Arts & Business Council of Greater Nashville, (615) 254-0030, vlaa.org.
