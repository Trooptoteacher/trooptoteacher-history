Commitment to Tennessee Compliance

TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC commits to maintaining Tennessee-aligned documentation and legal/compliance reviews throughout the life of this instructional materials package. Specifically:

  • Standards alignment documentation will be updated whenever TDOE revises the applicable standards set for U.S. History (US.01–US.95)
  • All legal/compliance reviews will be re-conducted if applicable Tennessee statutes are amended in ways that affect instructional content requirements
  • Schedule F self-assessment documentation will be updated following each round of revisions to reflect current materials state
  • District adoption compliance documentation (ED-5099 product information) will be maintained and updated as product information changes
  • TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC will respond to any reviewer, district, or TDOE inquiry regarding these materials within 5 business days

Until and unless a formal waiver is granted under applicable State Board rules, districts may use these materials only as supplemental instructional materials under TCA § 49-6-2202(a)(3) and current Tennessee law and waiver guidance.
