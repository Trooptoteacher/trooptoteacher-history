Both the ED-2153 (signed by the local board) and ED-5099 (list of adopted HQIMs) must be submitted electronically. Deadline for Schedule E cycle: June 30, 2026. What This Package Provides

This compliance package gives your district everything it needs to complete the adoption process for U.S. History: Reconstruction to Present by TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC. It includes:

  • Part A: Product Information for ED-5099 — pre-filled vendor data your district needs to complete the official TDOE form
  • Part B: ED-2153 Supporting Documentation — evidence your board needs to document its approval decision
  • Part C: ED-2154 Local Committee Checklist — documentation retained at the district level
  • Part D: Adoption Timeline and Submission Checklist — deadlines and submission steps
  • Part E: Standards Alignment Attestation — signed statement confirming 100% Tennessee standards coverage
Important Note on Official Forms The ED-2153, ED-5099, and ED-2154 are official TDOE forms to be completed and submitted by the district, not the vendor. The information in this package is provided to assist your district in accurately completing those forms. Obtain the official blank forms from the TDOE textbook adoption webpage or the submission links provided in the TDOE compliance notice. Questions: Academics.Instruction@tn.gov

Part C — ED-2154 Local Committee Documentation Checklist

The ED-2154 documents your district's local review committee process. This form is retained at the district level — it does not need to be submitted to TDOE. Use the checklist below to ensure your committee's process is fully documented before the June 30, 2026 deadline.

What ED-2154 Documents Your district's local review committee must examine the instructional materials against Tennessee academic standards and the TDOE Schedule F rubric criteria. Documentation should include: committee membership (with titles), dates of review sessions, rubric scores assigned by reviewers, and the basis for adoption recommendation.
  • Committee formed: At least one classroom teacher of U.S. History; one administrator; parent representative recommended
    Document names and titles of all committee members. A TTT pilot teacher from your district strengthens this documentation.
  • TTT materials reviewed against Schedule F rubric (all four tables)
    Table 1: Content Alignment  ·  Table 2: Instructional Focus  ·  Table 3: Social Studies Practices  ·  Table 4: Accessibility. Completed rubric available from vendor on request.
  • Tennessee standards alignment verified
    Standards Alignment Matrix on textbook site documents all 44 standards. Committee should verify at least a sample of standard-to-content mappings.
  • TCA § 49-6-1028(b) compliance verified — Virtues of capitalism and constitutional republic
    Present in Units 2 (Gilded Age/capitalism), 5 (New Deal debate), 9 (Reaganomics), and 10 (21st century economy). See Standards Matrix for specific standard codes and chapter locations.
  • TCA § 49-6-1006 compliance verified — Black history and culture
    Integrated throughout all 10 units: Reconstruction, Buffalo Soldiers, Harlem Renaissance, Tuskegee Airmen, Nashville Sit-ins, Civil Rights Acts, and more. Not siloed into a single section.
  • Tennessee-specific content verified
    Andrew Johnson · KKK founding Pulaski · Harry T. Burn · Scopes Trial · TVA · Oak Ridge · Cordell Hull · Nashville Sit-ins · Clinton High School · Tent City · Highlander Folk School · Lorraine Motel · Fort Campbell · Howard Baker · Al Gore · NAFTA & Tennessee. All present and sourced.
  • Committee review dates documented — at least one formal review meeting
    Record date(s), attendees, materials reviewed, rubric scores assigned, and recommendation made.
  • Recommendation to board prepared
    Written memo from committee to superintendent/board recommending adoption, summarizing evidence reviewed.
  • Board vote documented — with minutes reference
    Board must vote to approve. Record the meeting date, vote count, and board minutes reference number.

Part D — Adoption Timeline and Submission Checklist

April 2026
Contact TroopToTeacher Technologies — Request pilot access, digital review site, and full Schedule F documentation package. trooptoteacher.com
April–May 2026
Local committee review — Committee reviews materials against Schedule F rubric (all 4 tables) and Tennessee standards. Document committee composition, meeting dates, and rubric scores on ED-2154. File ED-2154 at the district level.
May 2026
Board vote — Present committee recommendation to the local board of education. Board votes to adopt. Document in board minutes. Board signs ED-2153.
By June 30, 2026
SUBMIT to TDOE — Submit signed ED-2153 and completed ED-5099 electronically using the TDOE Schedule E Adoption Reports Submission link. Questions: Academics.Instruction@tn.gov
After June 30
Late submissions — Contact TDOE directly for late submission guidance. T.C.A. § 49-6-2207(e) requires documentation upon completion of local adoption processes.
