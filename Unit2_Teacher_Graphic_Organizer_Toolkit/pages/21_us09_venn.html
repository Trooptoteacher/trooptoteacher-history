When · Why The era’s defining debate over how to advance African Americans. The overlap shows a shared goal, which sharpens the real difference in strategy — present both figures evenly and let students weigh the approaches. Identifying similarities & differences is Marzano’s highest-yield strategy.
Compare their strategies for African American advancement — background, education, and rights. Write what set each apart in its circle, and the goal they shared in the center.
Booker T. Washington W.E.B. Du Bois
★ Tennessee Connection. Fisk University in Nashville was central to this debate — W.E.B. Du Bois attended Fisk before he became a national voice for immediate rights.
Make it work for every student UDL · MTSS

Scaffold

Give a labeled word bank (Tuskegee, vocational, Atlanta Compromise, Talented Tenth, NAACP, Fisk, immediate rights) to sort into the regions.

Extend

Use the BOTH region to argue: if they shared the same goal, which strategy fit the moment — and what did each risk?

Show it your way

Students may write, say the contrast, draw a portrait pair, or build the Venn with sortable cards.
