When · Why Use to show how brutal industrial conditions — and a single tragedy, the 1911 Triangle Shirtwaist Factory Fire — drove the Progressive push for workplace reform. Analyzing cause & effect is TN Social Studies Practice SSP.05.
Harsh conditions on the left led to a tragedy in the center, which drove reform on the right. Arrows show the flow: causes → event → effects.
