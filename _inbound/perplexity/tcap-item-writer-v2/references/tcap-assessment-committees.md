# TCAP Assessment Committee Structure and Process (2025–2026)

Source: [TDOE Assessment Committees](https://www.tn.gov/education/districts/lea-operations/assessment/assessment-committees.html)

---

## Committee Types

### Item Review Committee

Reviews and approves assessment items for TCAP tests. Committee members evaluate items for content accuracy, alignment to Tennessee Academic Standards, bias/sensitivity, and adherence to item-writing conventions. Items that pass committee review are eligible for Pearson field testing and eventual operational use.

### Reading Passage Review Committee

Reviews reading passages and associated items for ELA assessments. Evaluates passage quality, complexity, cultural sensitivity, and alignment to standards.

### Rangefinding Committee

Reviews student work samples to calibrate scoring rubrics and establish benchmark responses for constructed-response and extended-response items.

### Performance Level Review Committee

Reviews and refines performance level descriptors (PLDs) that define what students should know and be able to do at each performance level (Below, Approaching, On Track, Mastered).

### Standard Setting Committee

Participates in the standard-setting process to establish cut scores for performance levels on new or revised assessments.

### Alignment Studies Committee

Reviews the alignment between assessment items and the Tennessee Academic Standards to ensure test validity and content coverage.

---

## Eligibility Requirements

- Must be a current Tennessee educator (teacher, instructional coach, curriculum specialist, or administrator)
- Content expertise in the relevant subject area and grade level
- Familiarity with Tennessee Academic Standards
- Commitment to attend the full duration of the assigned committee session
- Willingness to sign a confidentiality agreement regarding assessment content
- Prior assessment committee experience is valued but not required

---

## Stipend and Reimbursement

- Daily stipend provided for committee service
- Travel reimbursement for mileage, lodging, and meals per state guidelines
- Substitute teacher reimbursement available for districts (when applicable)
- Specific stipend amounts communicated upon selection

## Relevance to Item Writing

This skill targets the Item Review Committee for Social Studies, U.S. History, meeting June 2026 in Chattanooga as its quality standard. Every item produced by this skill should be written to survive the review process used by that committee:

1. Content accuracy verified against TN Academic Standards US.01–US.95
2. DOK and Bloom's classification defensible under committee scrutiny
3. Stem and option formatting compliant with TDOE conventions
4. Distractor quality sufficient to withstand item-level review
5. Bias/sensitivity screening completed before submission
6. IRT pre-calibration parameters estimated within target ranges
