# UDL 3.0 Design Contract Template

A design contract is a short, enforceable set of constraints that every piece of new content must satisfy before it ships. Attach one to every product line.

## Header

```
UDL Design Contract — [Product Line Name]
Version: [X.Y]
Framework: CAST UDL Guidelines 3.0 (2024)
Applies to: [What artifacts this covers]
Owner: [Person accountable for compliance]
```

## Section 1 — Firm Goals

State the standards, mastery outcomes, or firm learning targets that this product line must deliver. These are the ceilings that UDL protects. No overlay, scaffold, or modification touches these.

## Section 2 — Learner Variability Profile

Describe the full range of learners this product must serve:
- Grade bands
- EL levels (WIDA 1-6) and home languages
- IEP categories anticipated
- 504 accommodation categories anticipated
- GT / advanced learner range
- Cultural, linguistic, and socioeconomic diversity
- Assistive technology environments (screen readers, dictation, switches, print-to-braille, etc.)

## Section 3 — The 9 Design Musts

For every artifact in this product line, at least ONE consideration per guideline must be actively addressed. Below is the minimum bar; add more per artifact type.

### Engagement (WHY) — every artifact ships with:
- At least one **choice point** for learners (7.1)
- Content that connects to learner identities and lives (7.2 / 1.3)
- At least one **joy or curiosity anchor** (7.3) — not decoration; foundational
- **Goal clarity** at the start (8.1) — the WHY visible on the artifact itself
- **Feedback that is action-oriented** (8.5), not just a grade

### Representation (WHAT) — every artifact ships with:
- **Customizable display** where possible (1.1) — font size, contrast, spacing
- **Content available in more than one perception mode** (1.2) — text + audio, or text + visual, etc.
- **Vocabulary and language scaffolds** (2.1) — glossaries, cognates, sentence frames
- **Multiple perspectives** represented in content (1.3 / 3.3)
- **Prior knowledge activation** at the start (3.1)
- **Big idea, pattern, or organizing structure** made visible (3.2)

### Action & Expression (HOW) — every artifact ships with:
- **Multiple response modes** accepted (4.1 / 5.1) — written, oral, visual, gestural
- **AT compatibility** by default (4.2)
- **Graduated practice with scaffolds that fade** (5.3)
- **Organizing tools** for the learner (6.3) — templates, note structures, checklists
- **Progress monitoring** built in (6.4) — self-check questions, rubrics
- Explicit design that **challenges exclusionary practices** (6.5)

## Section 4 — On-Page Evidence Requirements

Every artifact ships with a visible **Provable Design Stamp** that shows:

- WHAT UDL considerations this design addresses (e.g., "1.1, 1.2, 2.1, 5.1, 6.3")
- HOW they are addressed (a 1-line description)
- FOR which learner variability (e.g., "WIDA Levels 2-4, IDEA 504 accommodations for extended time, GT extensions")
- EVIDENCE anchor (link to the CAST framework or product's UDL matrix)

The stamp is on the artifact itself. Not in a teacher guide. Not in a footnote. On the page.

## Section 5 — Non-Negotiables (Reject on These)

Reject any artifact that:

1. Uses "learning styles" language
2. Uses "at-risk" or deficit-framed labels for learners
3. Forces a single mode of response where an alternate mode could measure the same standard
4. Represents only a single dominant cultural perspective as neutral
5. Ships without a Provable Design Stamp
6. Contains a modification that lowers the ceiling on the standard (route to SPED, not UDL)
7. Contains bias, threat, or exclusion the design has not addressed (7.4, 2.4, 5.4, 6.5)

## Section 6 — Compliance Check

Before publishing:

- [ ] All 9 guidelines have at least one design consideration met
- [ ] Provable Design Stamp is on the artifact
- [ ] AT compatibility tested (screen reader pass, dictation pass, OpenDyslexic overlay available)
- [ ] Content reviewed for bias (2.4, 7.4)
- [ ] All firm goals preserved (no lowered ceilings)
- [ ] Learner variability profile explicitly addressed
- [ ] Artifact cites CAST 3.0 framework in the metadata

## Section 7 — Version & Attribution

```
Framework: CAST (2024). Universal Design for Learning Guidelines version 3.0.
Source: https://udlguidelines.cast.org/
Contract version: [X.Y]
Last review: [YYYY-MM-DD]
```
