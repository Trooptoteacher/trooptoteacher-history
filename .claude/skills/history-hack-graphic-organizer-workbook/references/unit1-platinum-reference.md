# Unit 1 — the platinum worked example

Unit 1 (Reconstruction & the Gilded Age · US.REC · US.01–US.07) is the gold standard. Every other unit
copies its structure, quality, and rigor. Use it as the reference build; the source lives in
`Unit1_Teacher_Graphic_Organizer_Toolkit/` (pack files in `src/`).

## The 24-page set
- **p1 — "Which Organizer, When?" Quick Guide** — task→organizer→why table, ≈Time column, and a
  "45-minute class?" pacing note (one deep organizer ~20 min, or two quick ones ~10 min each, plus
  warm-up & discussion). Tennessee Connection row highlighted gold as "our signature move."
- **p2 — SSP Crosswalk** — SSP.01–06 → organizers → where in Unit 1.
- **p3–17 — 15 blank reproducibles** (content-agnostic, reusable in any unit): Venn 2, Venn 3, T-chart,
  compare/contrast matrix, cause & effect, timeline, concept web, main idea & details, KWL, 5 Ws,
  problem–solution, Frayer, CER, HIPPO, Tennessee Connection. **Reuse these verbatim in every unit.**
- **p18–24 — 7 labeled best-fit organizers**, one per standard (below).

## The 7 labeled organizers (the per-standard pattern to replicate)
Each is the *best-fit* type for that standard's task, pre-loaded with sourced content, students fill light
areas, ★ TN flagged where a sourced tie exists.

- **US.01 — "Westward Expansion — Who Benefited, Who Bore the Cost"** (T-chart). Left: settlers/railroads/
  the growing nation (Homestead Act, Transcontinental RR, Buffalo Soldiers). Right: American Indian nations
  (land & treaties, way of life, sovereignty). ★ **George Jordan, Williamson County, TN** — Buffalo
  Soldier, 9th U.S. Cavalry, Medal of Honor. Neutral framing (not "winners/losers").
- **US.02 — "Federal Policy Toward American Indian Nations"** (numbered sequence / cause→effect):
  reservations → assimilation → boarding schools → **Dawes Act (1887)**, with a cause→effect reflection.
- **US.03 — "From a Disputed Election to *Plessy*"** (vertical timeline, date left): 1876 election →
  Compromise of 1877 → Jim Crow & disenfranchisement → **Plessy v. Ferguson (1896)**. ★ **T.C.A. § 49-6-1006**
  (Tennessee law requiring this instruction).
- **US.04 — "Three Forces of the Gilded Age"** (matrix): Farmers | Wage earners | Industrial capitalists ×
  criteria (what they wanted, biggest challenge, how they built power, where things stood by 1900).
- **US.05 — "Captain of Industry or Robber Baron?"** (T-chart, two lenses on the same figures: Carnegie,
  Rockefeller, Edison, Bell, Walker, Carver) + a "your verdict, because…" line.
- **US.06 — "Engines of Urban America"** (matrix, City × attribute): Boston, Chicago, New York City,
  Pittsburgh, San Francisco × signature industry / why it grew here / who powered it.
- **US.07 — "'Old' vs. 'New' Immigrants"** (Venn): pre-1880s N/W Europe vs. 1880s–1920s S/E Europe, shared
  immigrant experience in the overlap; region labels/hints kept inside the SVG.

## What "platinum" looked like in practice
- Real visual organizers (overlapping circles, arrow-linked chains, hub-and-spoke, quadrant+oval), never
  ruled-line tables.
- Light writable fields throughout; dark bands only for pre-filled labels.
- Every page: when·why blurb with a cited evidence hook, UDL·MTSS strip, Reproducible badge, time chip,
  TM/© footer; TN ties flagged.
- Neutral framing enforced (a review pass removed "winners," "win or a loss," and "who lost land").
- Rendered to a 24-page US-Letter PDF + 2× PNGs; imported to Canva (brand kit `kAG39-EGTcM`) as an
  editable design; everything committed to the repo and cross-linked from a Drive index doc.

## Reusing this for a new unit
1. Copy `Unit1_Teacher_Graphic_Organizer_Toolkit/` to `UnitN_.../`; keep `scripts/` and the blank-organizer
   packs unchanged (they're content-agnostic). Update the footer year only if the product year changes.
2. Rewrite the Quick Guide unit line + the SSP Crosswalk "where in Unit N" column, and author new labeled
   packs (`pack_20…`) — one per the unit's standards, best-fit type, sourced content, TN flagged.
3. Follow `sourcing.md` to align the labeled organizers to the unit's student workbook chapter, packets,
   and slide decks. Render + QC every page, export, and deliver as in `design-system.md`.
