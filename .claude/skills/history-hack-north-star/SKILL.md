# History Hack — North Star & Standing Decision Rule

> **Don't just learn history. Hack it.**
> _The curriculum that was built to make a difference, not to make money._

## The mission (why this exists)

**Pearson and McGraw-Hill were built to make money. TroopToTeacher Technologies and History Hack
were built to make a difference.** The product is never measured against what sells — it is measured
against what actually teaches. It must be **better, and more effective for students,** than anything
on the market. The bar is *better*, not parity.

## The decision rule (default answer to any "which way?" question)

When a scope, design, or trade-off question has a right answer, pick whatever maximizes, in order:

1. **100% alignment** — workbook ↔ teacher deck ↔ student deck ↔ standards, zero gaps. Every activity
   resolves to an exact slide; every taught segment has a home in every piece.
2. **TDOE Schedule F** — the Social Studies Instructional Materials Scoring Rubric is the ruler.
   Content accuracy (Policy 2.600) is foundational and non-negotiable. Score as-built, honestly.
3. **Best path to state adoption** — highest integrity, highest quality; what a TDOE reviewer *and*
   a real classroom teacher would both call the strongest material they've seen.

**Do not ask the user to arbitrate between these.** Default to the adoption-grade choice and proceed.
Only surface a question when it is a genuine fork that Schedule F and alignment do not settle — and
even then, lead with your recommendation, not an open-ended menu.

## How it shows up in the work

- **Accuracy is foundational.** Every date, number, name, statute, case, treaty, and figure verifiable
  to a primary/authoritative source. No known error ships. Unsure → cut it or caveat it.
- **Alignment is pass/fail.** A student follows the lesson with zero guesswork, or it isn't done.
- **Commercial-use-safe only** (PD / US-gov / CC0 / CC-BY; no share-alike, no permission-gated assets).
- **UDL 3.0 + MTSS built in** — supports add paths, never lower the ceiling, never replace an IEP/504.
- **Honesty doctrine** — report what's done, what's held, and every gap plainly. Never overclaim.
