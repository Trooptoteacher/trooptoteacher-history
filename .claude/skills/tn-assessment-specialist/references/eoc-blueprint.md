## Operational Items

- Total operational items per form: **47–52**
- Total items administered (including field test): **60**
- All Social Studies standards have an ideal point range of **0–4 points**
- Embedded field test items are included but do not count toward student scores

## Assessment Structure

- **Subparts**: 2
- **Subpart 1**: 30 items, 45 minutes
- **Subpart 2**: 30 items, 45 minutes
- **Total**: 60 items, 90 minutes

## Item Types

| Type | Options | Correct Answers | Points |
|------|---------|-----------------|--------|
| Multiple Choice (MC) | 4 answer options | 1 correct | 1 point |
| Multiple Select (MS) | >4 answer options | Multiple correct | 2 points (partial credit at ≥50%) |
| Technology Enhanced (TE) | Varies | Varies | 2 points (partial credit at ≥50%) |

## Blueprint Compliance Check Procedure

When generating an item set, calculate the percentage of items per reporting category and verify:

1. Each RC falls within its target range
2. Total operational items fall within 47–52
3. No single standard is over-represented relative to its RC weight
4. Field test items are flagged separately and excluded from RC % calculations

### Deviation Flags

- **WARN**: RC percentage within 2% of boundary
- **FAIL**: RC percentage outside target range
- **PASS**: RC percentage within target range with ≥2% margin
