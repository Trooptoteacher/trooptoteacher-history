# TCAP U.S. History EOC Assessment Overview

Revised: June 2025

Source: [TDOE USH EOC Assessment Overview (PDF)](https://www.tn.gov/content/dam/tn/education/testing/overviews/USH_EOC_Assessment_Overview.pdf)

---

## Test Structure

| Component | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Subparts** | 2 |
| **Items per Subpart** | 30 |
| **Time per Subpart** | 45 minutes |
| **Total Testing Time** | 90 minutes |
| **Total Items Administered** | 60 (operational + field test) |

---

## Item Types and Scoring

| Item Type | Description | Point Value | Partial Credit |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Multiple Choice (MC)** | Selected response with 4 options (A–D); 1 correct answer | 1 point | No |
| **Multiple Select (MS)** | Selected response with more than 4 options; 2 correct answers | 2 points | Yes — awarded at ≥50% correct selections |
| **Technology Enhanced (TE)** | Interactive item types (drag-and-drop, hot spot, matching, etc.) | 2 points | Yes — awarded at ≥50% correct interactions |

### Important Notes

- All TCAP EOC Social Studies assessments use **Selected Response** and **Technology Enhanced** item types only
- There are **no constructed-response, extended-response, or performance-based items** on the TCAP EOC
- Partial credit for MS and TE items is awarded when the student correctly completes at least 50% of the required interactions
- Field test items are embedded within the 60 total items but are **not scored** — they are used for psychometric calibration of future operational items

---

## Standards Assessed

Students are assessed on the **2019 Tennessee Academic Standards** for U.S. History and Geography:

- Standards US.01 through US.95
- Covering the period from Reconstruction through the Modern United States
- Organized into 5 reporting categories (RC1–RC5) aligned to the EOC Blueprint

## Implications for Item Writing

Understanding the EOC Assessment Overview is critical for item writing:

1. **Item types are limited**: Only MC, MS, and TE are used on the TCAP EOC. Items written as SA, CR, ER, or DBQ are classroom-only and must be tagged `tcap_format: false`.
2. **Partial credit matters**: MS and TE items award 2 points with partial credit at ≥50%. This affects distractor design — MS items should have clearly differentiated correct and incorrect options.
3. **Field test items are embedded**: The 60-item test includes both operational and field test items. Items written by this skill that pass committee review may enter the field test pool.
4. **Time constraints**: 45 minutes for 30 items = 1.5 minutes per item average. Stems should be clear and concise; avoid overly lengthy stimuli that consume reading time.
5. **2019 Standards**: All items must align to the 2019 TN Academic Standards (US.01–US.95), not any prior or draft versions.
