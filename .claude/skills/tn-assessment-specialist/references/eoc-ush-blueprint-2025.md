## Blueprint Overview

The TCAP EOC Blueprint for U.S. History and Geography defines the distribution of assessment items across five reporting categories. All standards have an **ideal point range of 0–4 points**, meaning any individual standard may contribute 0 to 4 points to a given test form.

| Technology Enhanced (TE) | 2 pts | Yes (≥50%) |

---

## Per-Standard Point Range

All standards carry an ideal point range of **0–4 points**. This means:

- A given standard may have 0 items on a particular test form (coverage rotates across forms)
- A standard may contribute up to 4 points through a combination of MC (1 pt each) and/or MS/TE (2 pts each)
- Not every standard is assessed on every form — the blueprint ensures proportional coverage at the reporting category level

---

## Blueprint Weight Compliance

When generating item sets, calculate the percentage of operational points allocated to each reporting category and compare against blueprint targets.

### Compliance Status Definitions

| Status | Condition |
|--------|-----------|
| **PASS** | RC percentage falls within the target range with ≥2% margin from boundaries |
| **WARN** | RC percentage is within 2% of the upper or lower boundary |
| **FAIL** | RC percentage falls outside the target range |

### Example Compliance Check (50 operational items)

| RC | Items | Points | Percentage | Target | Status |
|----|-------|--------|------------|--------|--------|
| RC1 | 9 | 10 | 18% | 14–22% | PASS |
| RC2 | 12 | 14 | 25% | 18–26% | WARN (within 2% of 26% ceiling) |
| RC3 | 11 | 13 | 23% | 18–26% | PASS |
| RC4 | 10 | 11 | 20% | 14–22% | PASS |
| RC5 | 8 | 8 | 14% | 10–18% | PASS |

## Reporting Category Detail

### RC1: 1877–1920 — Rise of Industrialization & Progressive Era (14–22%)

- **Units**: 1 (Rise of Industrialization) and 2 (Progressive Era)
- **Standards**: US.01–US.18 (18 standards)
- **Key Topics**: Big business, immigration, labor, urbanization, Populism, muckrakers, suffrage, Plessy v. Ferguson, Progressive presidents

### RC2: 1890–1929 — Imperialism, WWI & The 1920s (18–26%)

- **Units**: 3 (Imperialism and WWI) and 4 (The 1920s)
- **Standards**: US.19–US.38 (20 standards)
- **Key Topics**: Spanish-American War, imperialism debate, WWI causes/entry/outcome, Red Scare, Harlem Renaissance, consumer culture, immigration restriction

### RC3: 1929–1945 — Great Depression, New Deal & WWII (18–26%)

- **Units**: 5 (Great Depression and New Deal) and 6 (World War II)
- **Standards**: US.39–US.58 (20 standards)
- **Key Topics**: Causes of Depression, New Deal programs, TVA, totalitarianism, Pearl Harbor, home front, D-Day, Pacific Theater, atomic bomb, Holocaust

### RC4: 1947–1991 — Cold War & Nation in Transition (14–22%)

- **Units**: 7 (Cold War) and 8 (Nation in Transition)
- **Standards**: US.59–US.77 (19 standards)
- **Key Topics**: Containment, Korean War, McCarthyism, Space Race, Cuban Missile Crisis, Vietnam, Watergate, Reaganomics, Great Society

### RC5: 1950s–Present — Civil Rights & Modern US (10–18%)

- **Units**: 9 (Civil Rights Movement) and 10 (Modern United States)
- **Standards**: US.78–US.95 (18 standards)
- **Key Topics**: Brown v. Board, Montgomery Bus Boycott, Civil Rights Act, Voting Rights Act, Black Power, women's/Chicano/AIM movements, 9/11, War on Terror, globalization
