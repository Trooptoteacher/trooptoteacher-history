# TDOE Social Studies Scoring Rubric — Detailed Indicator Tables

Load this reference when performing Stage 4 (Rubric Scoring). Score every indicator below using the 0-1-2 scale.

## Scoring Scale

| Score | Label | Criteria |
|-------|-------|----------|
| 0 | Not Evident / Not Aligned | Indicator is absent, irrelevant, or fundamentally misaligned with TN standards. No credible evidence found in the material. |
| 1 | Partially Evident / Partially Aligned | Indicator is present but incomplete, superficial, or inconsistently applied. Some evidence exists but gaps remain. |
| 2 | Clearly Evident / Fully Aligned | Indicator is thoroughly addressed with strong, consistent evidence throughout the material. Meets or exceeds TN expectations. |

---

## Table 1: Alignment of Content

This table assesses standards-by-standards alignment depth. For each TN standard (US.01–US.95 for U.S. History), evaluate:

| Indicator | What to Look For |
|-----------|-----------------|
| 1.1 Standard Coverage | Content directly addresses the standard's knowledge and skill expectations |
| 1.2 Depth of Treatment | Standard is covered with sufficient depth, not just surface mention |
| 1.3 Accuracy | Content is factually accurate and reflects current historiography |
| 1.4 Sequence Alignment | Content appears in a logical sequence matching TN pacing expectations |
| 1.5 Assessment Alignment | End-of-section/chapter assessments measure the standard being taught |

**Scoring method:** Score each standard group (by unit/reporting category) on indicators 1.1–1.5. Calculate subtotal as: `(points earned across all standards × indicators) / (total possible points) × 100`.

## Table 2: Instructional Focus (9 Indicators)

| # | Indicator | Score 0 | Score 1 | Score 2 |
|---|-----------|---------|---------|---------|
| 2.1 | **Rigor** — Content requires students to engage with complex texts, analyze evidence, and construct arguments | No rigorous tasks; recall-only | Some rigorous tasks but inconsistent across units | Consistent rigor; DOK 3-4 tasks in every unit |
| 2.2 | **Higher-Order Thinking** — Activities move beyond recall to analysis, evaluation, and synthesis (Bloom's upper levels) | Activities are overwhelmingly recall/comprehension | Mix of lower and higher-order; higher-order in < 50% of activities | Higher-order thinking embedded in ≥ 50% of activities |
| 2.3 | **Primary Sources** — Authentic primary source documents are integrated into instruction, not just supplementary | No primary sources or only decorative use | Some primary sources but not systematically integrated | Primary sources in every unit with scaffolded analysis tasks |
| 2.4 | **Vocabulary** — Academic and domain-specific vocabulary is explicitly taught, practiced, and assessed | No vocabulary instruction | Vocabulary listed but not taught or assessed | Vocabulary explicitly taught with practice, context, and assessment |
| 2.5 | **Differentiation** — Materials provide multiple entry points, scaffolds, and extensions for diverse learners | One-size-fits-all; no differentiation | Some differentiation (e.g., reading levels) but limited | Comprehensive differentiation: scaffolds, extensions, ELL support, accommodations |
| 2.6 | **Assessment** — Formative and summative assessments aligned to standards with varied item types | No assessments or misaligned assessments | Assessments present but limited variety or weak alignment | Robust assessment suite: formative + summative, multiple item types, standards-aligned |
| 2.7 | **Pacing** — Scope and sequence provides realistic pacing for a full academic year | No pacing guide or unrealistic timeline | Pacing guide exists but has significant gaps or compression | Realistic pacing for 180-day year with built-in review and assessment windows |
| 2.8 | **Teacher Support** — Teacher edition provides pedagogical guidance, answer keys, discussion prompts, and extension ideas | No teacher edition or minimal guidance | Teacher edition exists but lacks depth | Comprehensive teacher edition with pedagogy notes, answer keys, discussion guides, and remediation strategies |
| 2.9 | **Digital Integration** — Digital components enhance learning (not just digitized text) and function reliably | No digital component or non-functional | Digital exists but adds minimal instructional value | Digital components are interactive, functional, and enhance instruction beyond print |

**Table 2 subtotal:** `(sum of 9 indicator scores) / 18 × 100`

## Table 3: Social Studies Practices / SSPs (7 Indicators)

These align with the C3 Framework and Tennessee's Social Studies Practices.

| # | Indicator | Score 0 | Score 1 | Score 2 |
|---|-----------|---------|---------|---------|
| 3.1 | **Sourcing** — Students identify author, purpose, audience, and context of sources | No sourcing activities | Occasional sourcing prompts | Systematic sourcing tasks with every primary/secondary source |
| 3.2 | **Contextualization** — Students place events, people, and ideas within historical context | Events presented in isolation | Some contextualization but inconsistent | Consistent contextualization connecting events to broader patterns |
| 3.3 | **Corroboration** — Students compare multiple sources to verify claims and identify perspectives | Single-source treatment of topics | Occasional multi-source comparison | Regular corroboration tasks with 2+ sources per key topic |
| 3.4 | **Close Reading** — Students engage in careful, analytical reading of complex texts | No close reading instruction | Some close reading but no structured protocol | Structured close reading protocols with annotation and guided questions |
| 3.5 | **Argumentation** — Students construct evidence-based arguments and evaluate competing claims | No argumentation tasks | Some argument writing but without structured evidence use | Regular argumentation tasks with claim-evidence-reasoning structure |
| 3.6 | **Economic Reasoning** — Students apply economic concepts (cost-benefit, supply-demand, trade) to historical analysis | No economic reasoning | Economic concepts mentioned but not applied analytically | Economic reasoning integrated into historical analysis where relevant |
| 3.7 | **Geographic Reasoning** — Students use spatial thinking and geographic concepts in historical analysis | No geographic reasoning or maps | Maps/geography present but decorative | Geographic reasoning actively integrated (cause-effect of geography on events) |

**Table 3 subtotal:** `(sum of 7 indicator scores) / 14 × 100`

## Table 4: Accessibility Features (5 Indicators)

| # | Indicator | Score 0 | Score 1 | Score 2 |
|---|-----------|---------|---------|---------|
| 4.1 | **ADA Compliance** — Digital materials meet WCAG 2.1 AA or higher; print materials have adequate font, contrast, and layout | Non-compliant; major barriers | Partially compliant; some barriers remain | Fully ADA compliant; no identified barriers |
| 4.2 | **NIMAS Compliance** — Materials available in National Instructional Materials Accessibility Standard format | No NIMAS file provided | NIMAS file provided but incomplete | Full NIMAS-compliant file set available |
| 4.3 | **UDL Principles** — Materials apply Universal Design for Learning (multiple means of engagement, representation, action/expression) | No UDL consideration | Some UDL elements but limited | Comprehensive UDL implementation across all three principles |
| 4.4 | **Multilingual Support** — Materials provide support for English learners (glossaries, translated resources, visual supports) | No EL support | Basic EL support (glossary only) | Robust EL support: bilingual glossaries, visual aids, scaffolded language |
| 4.5 | **Assistive Technology Compatibility** — Digital materials work with screen readers, text-to-speech, alternative input devices | Incompatible with assistive tech | Partially compatible; some features inaccessible | Fully compatible; tested with major assistive technologies |

**Table 4 subtotal:** `(sum of 5 indicator scores) / 10 × 100`

## Overall Score Calculation

```
Overall Score = (Table 1 points + Table 2 points + Table 3 points + Table 4 points)
                / (Table 1 max + Table 2 max + Table 3 max + Table 4 max) × 100
```

Report as a percentage rounded to one decimal place.

**Threshold application:**
- ≥ 80%: Eligible for RECOMMENDED (if gateways passed)
- 60–79.9%: REVISE AND RESUBMIT
- < 60%: NOT RECOMMENDED
