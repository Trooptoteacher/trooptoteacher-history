What it was
ended trade barriers among the U.S., Canada & Mexico
work requirements & time limits replaced older welfare (TANF)
growth & tax increases erased the federal deficit
the Lewinsky affair → charges of perjury & obstruction
Outcome
trade rose — but many manufacturing jobs moved to Mexico
welfare rolls fell sharply; poverty reduction was limited
budget surpluses — a strong late‑1990s economy
the House impeached; the Senate acquitted — a party‑line split
★ Tennessee Connection — Vice President Al Gore of Carthage, Tennessee, shaped the administration’s technology policy and the “information superhighway.” He later won the Nobel Peace Prize for his environmental advocacy.
