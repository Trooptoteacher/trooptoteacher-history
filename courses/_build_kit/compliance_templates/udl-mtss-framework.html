Universal Design for Learning (3.0) & MTSS — Framework Alignment

Foundations of Constitutional Government · United States Government & Civics (GC) · District adoption defensibility
The barrier is in the design of the environment, not the learner. Accommodations are designed in by default, not retrofitted. Firm goals (TN GC standards + SSPs), flexible means. This document maps the course's design decisions to the CAST UDL Guidelines version 3.0 (all nine guidelines, thirty considerations) and to a three-tier MTSS model. UDL and MTSS are built in as frameworks — largely invisible on the page to teacher and student, but explicit and reviewable here. Firm goals, flexible means: the means vary; the TN GC standard and Social Studies Practices never do.

Principle 1 — Engagement (the WHY of learning)

Multiple Means of Engagement — the WHY of learning (affective networks).

7.4 Address Biases Threats DistractionsDesigned in
  • Neutral-framing guardrail: no loaded language; ‘Who benefited / Who bore the cost’ instead of winners/losers.
  • Judgment is left to the student’s response line, never pre-decided in the design.
8. Sustaining Effort Persistence8.1 Clarify Meaning And Purpose Of GoalsDesigned in
  • Verbatim ‘I can’ learning target on every standard.
  • Explicit success criteria (3-part) and cues per standard.
8.2 Optimize Challenge And SupportDesigned in
  • Scaffold + Extend columns on every organizer (Tier 2 support and enrichment on the same page).
  • DOK range 1–4 per standard; ‘choose your challenge’ CER levels at one firm standard.
8.3 Collaboration InterdependenceDesigned in
  • Partner-first and ‘talk-first’ scaffolds; discuss options; card-sort collaborative moves in scaffolds.
8.4 Foster Belonging And CommunityDesigned in
  • ‘Whose Voice Counted / Whose Participation Counts’ multiple-perspectives sections surface historically excluded voices.
  • Bilingual EN/ES presence signals belonging for multilingual learners.
8.5 Action Oriented FeedbackDesigned in
  • Check-for-Understanding with answer rationale (the ‘why’), not just a key.
  • Progress self-check (‘how sure are you?’); teacher-side reteach; mastery analytics feed next steps.
9. Emotional Capacity9.1 Recognize Expectations Beliefs MotivationsDesigned in
  • A predict-first belief-check opens every unit ('Make It Stick' slide + content data), surfacing prior beliefs before instruction.
  • Contract-enforced for Units 3–7.
9.2 Awareness Of Self And OthersDesigned in
  • Multiple-perspectives framing builds awareness of others’ viewpoints (SSP.03).
  • Historical-empathy prompts (SSP.05) ask students to view events as people of the time.
9.3 Individual And Collective ReflectionDesigned in
  • CER and ‘your verdict, because…’ reflection lines; progress checks; unit essential-question revisit slide.
9.4 Empathy And Restorative PracticesDesigned in
  • Per-unit discussion norms (Unit 1 'Constitutional Conversations'; Unit 2 'Civic Conversations') set restorative, evidence-first dialogue rules.
  • Perspective-taking and 'who was excluded' framing build empathy.

MTSS — Multi-Tiered System of Supports

Supports are designed into the core materials, tiered by need, and driven by progress-monitoring data.

TierRoleEvidence in the course build
Tier 1 UniversalThe platinum core instruction itself is Tier 1: UDL-embedded, all students, firm TN GC standard + SSP goals.
  • Every lesson is print + digital, bilingual, neutral-framed, with response choice and success criteria.
  • Primary-source-based inquiry with graphic-organizer support for all learners.
Tier 2 TargetedSame standard, added support for learners who need it — designed into the page, not a separate worksheet.
  • Scaffold column: sentence frames, word banks, partner-first, card-sorts.
  • Support-form (A–B) assessment items keep the correct answer + one strong distractor.
  • Frayer vocabulary and pronunciation guides for language demand.
Tier 3 IntensiveMost-scaffolded pathway and teacher-directed reteach for learners needing intensive support.
  • Teacher-side reteach / priority-teaching guidance in the Teacher Guide.
  • Most-supported response mode (say/draw/build) available at the same standard.
  • 1:1 conferring prompts and re-teach triggers surfaced by the mastery model.
Progress MonitoringThe data layer that drives MTSS tier decisions.
  • Per-standard Check-for-Understanding with DOK tags.
  • Mastery analytics: standard-level mastery, DOK 3–4 weighting, remediation triggers, spiral interleaving.
  • Formative question banks with psychometric metadata for pre-field-test analysis.

Design Contract (held on every future unit)

  • Every activity offers a firm goal (the TN GC standard + SSP) and at least two flexible means to reach it.
  • Every writable task offers response choice (write / say / draw / build).
  • Every vocabulary term carries a plain-language definition, pronunciation, and an EN/ES gloss.
  • Every image carries bilingual alt text; every page is print + digital, B&W-safe, WCAG-AA.
  • Every page carries a Scaffold (Tier 2) and an Extend option without lowering the standard’s ceiling.
  • Framing is neutral in titles, labels, prompts, and criteria; judgment is left to the student.
  • Multiple perspectives and historically excluded voices are named, not flattened into one ‘neutral’ narrative.
  • Reduced-choice and language supports are access formats at the same standard — never IEP modification labeled as UDL.

Adoption Defensibility

PopulationHow the design meets it
IEPAccommodations (extended response modes, read-aloud, reduced-choice access format, graphic-organizer support) are designed into the core materials by default, satisfying least-restrictive-environment expectations without a separate retrofit.
504Access features (high-contrast print, digital reflow, AT-compatible digital, pronunciation and language supports) are universal, not conditional on a plan.
WIDABilingual EN/ES vocabulary and alt text, sentence frames, word banks, and multimodal representation support multilingual learners across WIDA levels while holding the same content standard.
GTExtend options, DOK 3–4 tasks, honors prompts, and open inquiry raise the ceiling for gifted/talented learners within the same lesson.
The UDL / modification boundary: UDL never lowers the ceiling on a TN GC standard or drops an SSP. Anything that would change the skill assessed is IEP modification and is routed separately, not labeled UDL.
