## Unit 1 — Foundations of Constitutional Government · GC.01–GC.09
**Essential question:** How and why did the Founders build a limited constitutional republic?
**Spotlight (Founding-Document):** Declaration of Independence · Articles of Confederation ·
Federalist No. 10 & No. 51 · Anti-Federalist (Brutus No. 1) · Preamble · Bill of Rights.
**Lead SSPs:** SSP.01 (compelling/supporting questions) · SSP.02 (gather/evaluate sources) · SSP.03 (corroborate).
**Lead civic labels:** `[CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES]` `[CIVIC ARGUMENTATION]`
**TCA flag:** **GC.08 — Bill of Rights — legally required (T.C.A. § 49-6-1028).**

## Unit 2 — The Legislative Branch · GC.10–GC.15
**Essential question:** How does Article I make Congress both powerful and accountable?
**Spotlight (Document + Case):** Article I + 17th Amendment · **Baker v. Carr (1962)** (redistricting/apportionment).
**Lead SSPs:** SSP.04 (construct arguments) · SSP.05 (communicate conclusions) · SSP.06 (geographic/historical awareness — census & redistricting).
**Lead civic labels:** `[SEPARATION OF POWERS / CHECKS & BALANCES]` `[FEDERALISM]`
**TCA flags:** **GC.11** (census/redistricting/Baker v. Carr) and **GC.14** (TN delegation) — T/TCA legally required.

## Unit 3 — The Executive Branch · GC.16–GC.18
**Essential question:** What are the powers and limits of the presidency?
**Spotlight (Document):** Article II · 22nd & 25th Amendments · Electoral College clauses (Art. II §1 / 12th Amд).
**Lead SSPs:** SSP.02 · SSP.04 (Electoral College debate as structured argument).
**Lead civic labels:** `[SEPARATION OF POWERS / CHECKS & BALANCES]` `[CIVIC ARGUMENTATION]`

## Unit 4 — The Judicial Branch · GC.19–GC.22
**Essential question:** How did the courts gain the power to say what the Constitution means?
**Spotlight (Case):** Article III · **Marbury v. Madison (1803)** (judicial review) · constitutionality of laws & acts.
**Lead SSPs:** SSP.03 (corroborate) · SSP.04 (argue) · SSP.05 (communicate).
**Lead civic labels:** `[CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLES]` `[SEPARATION OF POWERS / CHECKS & BALANCES]`

## Unit 5 — Civil Liberties · GC.23–GC.27
**Essential question:** How do the Bill of Rights and the 14th Amendment protect individual liberty?
**Spotlight (Landmark Cases):** 1st Amд cases · 2nd Amд · **Mapp v. Ohio**, **Gideon v. Wainwright**,
**Miranda v. Arizona** (4th–8th) · **Plessy v. Ferguson**, **Brown v. Board**, **Gitlow v. New York**,
**Roe v. Wade**, **Obergefell v. Hodges** (14th / due process / equal protection) · Title IX & ADA.
**Lead SSPs:** SSP.02 · SSP.03 · SSP.04 (rights argumentation).
**Lead civic labels:** `[RIGHTS ANALYSIS]` `[CIVIC ARGUMENTATION]`
**TCA flags:** **GC.23–GC.27** — T/TCA legally required.
