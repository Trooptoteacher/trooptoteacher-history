# Accessibility & Accessible-Formats Statement — Foundations of Constitutional Government

This statement backs the "large-print / screen-reader friendly" line in the UDL access strips with evidence, and states honestly what is in place versus on the roadmap. It maps to CAST UDL 3.0 Guideline 1 (Perception) and 2 (Language & Symbols).

## In place today (verified in the artifacts)
- **Alternate text on every image.** Each primary-source image in the Student Workbooks carries a Word `descr` alt-text drawn from its bilingual caption (verified: image count = alt-text count in every unit). Decks carry `alt` on each embedded source.
- **Bilingual access (EN/ES).** Every vocabulary term ships with a Spanish gloss (`es`) and a pronunciation respelling (`say`) — Representation 2.1/2.2/2.3.
- **Reflowable, zoomable source.** Deliverables are Word `.docx` (reflowable, screen-reader-navigable by heading structure) and tagged-heading PDFs from the decks; text is plain and read-aloud-compatible.
- **Multiple means of expression.** Every standard's constructed-response (CER) task now offers write / say-and-record / draw-and-label / build modes against the *same* rubric, plus word-processor, speech-to-text, scribe, and sentence-frame provisions (Action & Expression 4.1/5.1/5.2/5.3).
- **Structured headings + Table of Contents** in every workbook and assessment book for screen-reader navigation (Perception 1.1 customize/navigate).

## Available on request
- **Accessible / large-print formats** of any Student Workbook are available on request; the source is structured for large-print reflow (increase the base style size) and screen-reader use.

## Documented roadmap (not yet complete — stated so we do not over-claim)
- **Full PDF/UA reading-order tagging** of the exported PDFs (docx heading structure is present; explicit tagged reading order + table markup is the next accessibility milestone).
- **A dedicated large-print edition build** (a `LARGEPRINT` generation flag producing a 16-pt reflowed workbook) rather than on-request scaling.
- **WCAG 2.2 AA review** of the web edition surfaces.

## Standard we hold to
Accessible formats provide the **same standard** and the **same ceiling** — they change access and expression, never the mastery target. Where the source cannot yet deliver a format, it is listed above as roadmap rather than asserted as done. _CAST (2024). Universal Design for Learning Guidelines version 3.0._
