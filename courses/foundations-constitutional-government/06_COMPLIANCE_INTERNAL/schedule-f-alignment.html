Summary & Next Steps

Gateway MET. With all seven units fully built, the course scores 2 on every Table 1 standard, 2 across all of Table 2 and Table 4, and 2 on five of six SSPs. The single honest gap is SSP.06 (geographic awareness) — scored 1 because spatial reasoning is concentrated in the TN State & Local unit and jurisdictional content rather than appearing in every lesson, which is inherent to a Government & Civics discipline.
