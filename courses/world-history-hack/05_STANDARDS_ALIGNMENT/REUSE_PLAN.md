# Reuse-First Plan — pull existing U.S. History resources before authoring

**INTERNAL.** For each World History standard with US overlap, harvest the existing US resources (question items, textbook narrative, packets) *first*, then reframe to the World History I-can and de-leak. Standards with no US overlap are authored fresh from the verbatim standard + Phase-2 primary source.

- Strong overlap (reuse items + scaffolds, reframe lens): **28** standards
- Partial (reuse selectively + extend to WH scope): **22**
- Thematic (reuse framing/skills only): **7**
- Author fresh (no US overlap): **35**
- Existing US question items harvestable (strong+partial): **~422**
