## Cross-cutting — Social Studies Practices (all units)
- **SSP.01** Collect data and information from a variety of primary and secondary sources.
- **SSP.02** Critically examine primary or secondary sources.
- **SSP.03** Synthesize data from a variety of sources.
- **SSP.04** Construct and communicate arguments citing supporting evidence.
- **SSP.05** Develop historical awareness.
- **SSP.06** Develop geographic awareness.
