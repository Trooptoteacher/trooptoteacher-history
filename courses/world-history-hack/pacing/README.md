# World History & Geography — Pacing Guide (2026–27)

`HH_World_History_Pacing_Guide_202627.docx` — full-course pacing guide for World History & Geography,
built to match the U.S. History Hack pacing-guide layout.

## What it covers
- **Calendar:** Williamson County Schools 2026–2027, 177 instructional days across 4 quarters
  (44 / 39 / 46 / 48). Late-start (⏰) and half-day (½) marks and district breaks match the WCS calendar.
- **Standards:** all 89 TDOE World History & Geography standards (W.01–W.89) in framework order,
  grouped into 8 color-coded units.
- **Assessments:**
  - *Summative* (shaded amber): an end-of-unit **CSA / Unit Test** for every unit on a locked date,
    plus **Fall (Dec 15–18)** and **Spring (May 24–27)** Semester Exams, and district BOY & Q1–Q3 Benchmarks.
  - *Formative* (shaded green): a scheduled **mid-unit progress check** in every unit, plus DBQ, Inquiry,
    and Primary-Source task days.
- **End of year:** World History & Geography is not state EOC-tested, so the spring term closes with
  course review, a Contemporary-Global-Issues capstone, and semester exams.
