United States History and Geography 

 Social Studies Instructional Materials Scoring Rubric 

Gateway: The publisher must provide a Tennessee standards alignment guide as a part of the scope and sequence for the material. If this gateway is not 

met, the materials will not be scored. All Tennessee standards must be addressed within the material. If this is not met, the material will not pass review by 

the Tennessee Textbook and Instructional Materials Quality Commission. 

Introduction: 

The following Instructional Materials Scoring Rubric for Social Studies is designed to score materials in the following categories:  

•  Alignment of Content 
• 
Instructional Focus 
•  Social Studies Practices 
•  Accessibility Features 

Scoring:  

Each section is to be scored using a 0, 1, or 2. Use the following scoring guideline.  

Tables 1-4:  

•  0: The standard is not present within the material. 
•  1: The standard is present within the material. The intent and /or frequency component of the standard is not fully met. 
•  2: A rating of 2 indicates the standard is present and all aspects of the standard are fully met.
