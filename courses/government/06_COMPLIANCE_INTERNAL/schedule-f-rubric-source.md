# TDOE Schedule F — Social Studies Instructional Materials Scoring Rubric (source of truth)

> Provenance: author-supplied TDOE rubric PDF (`Schedule_F_Social_Studies…US_History.pdf`). Verbatim
> criteria captured for the Government course QC. The published standard list in the source is US History;
> for this course the **Table 1** standard rows are replaced by the verbatim **GC.01–GC.35** standards
> (see `05_STANDARDS_ALIGNMENT/government_standards_source.json`). Tables 2–4 criteria are course-agnostic.

## Gateway (pass/fail — if unmet, materials are NOT scored)
The publisher must provide a **Tennessee standards alignment guide** as part of the **scope and sequence**.
**All** Tennessee standards must be addressed within the material.

## Scoring (Tables 1–4): 0 / 1 / 2
- **0** — the standard/criterion is **not present** in the material.
- **1** — **present**, but the **intent and/or frequency** component is **not fully met**.
- **2** — **present and all aspects fully met**.

## Table 1 — Alignment of Content
**Conceptual Understanding:** Materials support student engagement with the Tennessee Social Studies
standards and model curriculum, providing a clear, standards-based pathway to address content and skills
in a coherent, sequenced manner. *(Scored per standard.)*

## Table 2 — Instructional Focus
- **Inquiry questions:** open-ended, thought-provoking questions that encourage inquiry and align with the
  big ideas; clear, scaffolded supporting questions build toward the compelling question.
- **Student-centered approach:** promotes inquiry, choice, and collaboration; supports exploration, critical
  thinking, and real-world problem-solving; students consistently engage in authentic disciplinary practices
  through inquiry, primary source analysis, and evidence-based writing.
- **Disciplinary Literacy:** meaningful engagement with disciplinary thinking (not rote memorization).
- **Multiple Perspectives and Narratives:** meaningful inclusion of multiple perspectives integrated into narratives.
- **Concepts before Vocabulary:** conceptual understanding is prioritized; vocabulary is introduced **after**
  students explore ideas.
- **Connections across content ideas:** connections across time, themes, or disciplines.

## Table 3 — Attending to the Social Studies Practices (SSPs) — 0/1/2 each
- Collect information from various primary and secondary sources (SSP.01)
- Critically examine primary & secondary sources (SSP.02)
- Synthesize data from multiple sources (SSP.03)
- Construct and communicate ideas supported by evidence (SSP.04)
- Develop historical awareness (SSP.05)
- Develop geographic awareness (SSP.06)

## Table 4 — Accessibility Features
- **Digital Materials:** all lessons available in digital form **and** include a printable option.
- **SWD/ELL supports:** in **every lesson**, recommended supports, accommodations, and modifications for
  Students with Disabilities and English learners that support regular, active participation on grade-level
  material (e.g., modifying vocabulary, sentence starters).
