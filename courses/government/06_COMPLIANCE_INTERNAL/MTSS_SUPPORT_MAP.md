## How the tiers are triggered
- **Tier 1 → Tier 2:** a missed formative checkpoint or a wrong distractor on the Practice Quiz/CER routes to the specific misconception (see each Assessment Book **Part 6** and the item bank `remediation.by_distractor`).
- **Tier 2 → Tier 3:** the same misconception persists across **parallel forms** (generate with `08_QUESTION_BANK/generate_parallel_tests.py`).
- Progress is monitored on the **same standard** at each tier — re-assessment always uses an equated parallel item, never an easier one.
