# Product Naming Convention (standing rule)

**Company / owner:** TroopToTeacher Technologies, LLC
**Trademark status:** common-law **™** (not federally registered yet — registration in motion). Use **™**, never **®**, until federal registration is granted.
**Copyright line:** © 2026 TroopToTeacher Technologies, LLC.

## Product family

| Product | Subject | Mark |
|---|---|---|
| **U.S. History Hack** | U.S. History content (US.01–US.95) | U.S. History Hack™ |
| **World History Hack** | World History content | World History Hack™ |
| **Government Hack** | Government content | Government Hack™ |

## Default rule (apply unless told otherwise)

- **Unless the user explicitly says they are working on World History Hack or Government Hack, everything — the product and all U.S. History content — is identified as "U.S. History Hack."**
- All **newly generated materials** (lesson plans, print packs, generated deliverables) and **go-forward** work use **U.S. History Hack™**.
- The **existing shipped app UI and already-distributed docs** keep their current "History Hack" wording for now (go-forward rename only; no blind retroactive find-replace). World History / Government content is left under its own naming.
- The domain `ushistoryhack.com` / `app.ushistoryhack.com` already matches "U.S. History Hack."

## Embedding on generated materials
- Visible footer: `© 2026 TroopToTeacher Technologies, LLC · U.S. History Hack™ · All rights reserved.`
- File metadata: Author/Company = TroopToTeacher Technologies, LLC; copyright in description/keywords.
