Validation Methodology The frequency data in this table is drawn directly from the Tennessee Instructional Playbook for U.S. History and Geography (2025–2026), which documents the number of TCAP U.S. History End-of-Course items addressing each standard in each administration year from 2021 through 2025. A shaded circle (•) indicates the standard appeared on that year’s EOC; an open circle indicates it did not. This data allows prioritization of question bank use: standards appearing 4–5 of the last 5 years are the highest-probability items for student preparation. All 44 standards with EOC frequency data are covered in the TroopToTeacher question bank with 10 questions each.

Key Finding — For Tennessee Textbook Commission Reviewers Every Tennessee Academic Standard (US.01–US.44) that appeared on the TCAP U.S. History End-of-Course assessment in any of the five administrations from 2021 through 2025 is covered in the TroopToTeacher Technologies question bank with 10 aligned questions per standard. This represents 44 of 44 standards — 100% coverage of the EOC-tested content range. The two standards with the highest EOC frequency over five years (US.39: Causes of the Great Depression and US.43: New Deal Programs including the TVA) are both covered with full 10-question sets including primary source DBQs, constructed responses, and extended responses at DOK levels 1–3.
Tennessee-Specific Content Note Seven standards in this table contain Tennessee-required content flagged under TCA § 49-6-1006 and the Tennessee Academic Standards. These include: US.01 (George Jordan, Williamson County), US.03 (Pap Singleton and Exodusters), US.11 (Coal Creek Labor Saga, Anderson County), US.18 (Tennessee “Perfect 36,” Harry T. Burn), US.25 (Alvin C. York), US.30 (Grand Ole Opry, W.C. Handy), and US.36 (Scopes Trial, Dayton, Rhea County). All Tennessee-specific questions are tagged and filterable in the interactive question bank browser.
Standards US.45–US.95 — In Production The frequency table above covers the content range documented in the 2025–2026 Tennessee Instructional Playbook for the first two quarters of the U.S. History course (US.01–US.44). Standards US.45–US.95 covering World War II, the Cold War, Civil Rights, Vietnam, and Modern U.S. history also appear on the TCAP EOC and are represented in the textbook units. Assessment questions for these standards are in active production and will be submitted as complete sets. EOC frequency data for US.45–US.95 will be validated against the same source document upon completion.
