COURSE OVERVIEW

Course Title: United States History: Reconstruction to Present Grade Level: 11th Grade (primary); dual-enrollment compatible Course Code: [VERIFY with TDOE — check current course catalog code] Total Instructional Days: 170 (leaves 10 flex/review/testing days in 180-day year) Textbook Series: History Hack U.S. History | TroopToTeacher Technologies Alignment: Tennessee Academic Standards for Social Studies (current, in effect until 2027-28) AND Tennessee Revised Social Studies Standards (adopted February 2024, implementation 2027-28)

Course Description: United States History: Reconstruction to Present examines the political, economic, social, and cultural development of the United States from the end of the Civil War through the contemporary era. Students engage in authentic historical inquiry — asking questions, analyzing primary and secondary sources, evaluating evidence, and constructing arguments — while developing the historical awareness, geographic understanding, and civic literacy necessary for participation in a constitutional democracy. The course fulfills requirements under TCA § 49-6-1202 (American history and government), TCA § 49-6-1028(b) (virtues of capitalism and constitutional republic), and addresses TCA § 49-6-1006 (Black history and culture) comprehensively throughout Units 7 and across multiple other units.

SOCIAL STUDIES PRACTICES (SSPs) OVERVIEW

All six SSPs are embedded across all ten units. Each unit identifies a PRIMARY SSP (the one most deeply scaffolded throughout that unit) and SECONDARY SSPs.
