When · Why Reading it as roots → flashpoint → crises shows how a revolution 90 miles away led, step by step, to the most dangerous standoff of the Cold War. Analyzing cause & effect is TN Social Studies Practice SSP.05.
The roots of conflict sit on the left; four consequences flow out on the right. Arrows show the chain: roots → flashpoint → crises — ending at the closest brush with nuclear war.
Causes · roots of conflict
The U.S. had dominated Cuba since 1898 & backed dictator Batista
Castro’s 1959 revolution — then communism & nationalization
Castro allies with the USSR — a communist state 90 miles from Florida
The flashpoint
CUBA: A COLD WAR FLASHPOINT
90 miles from Florida
A communist ally of the USSR on America’s doorstep — describe why that alarmed the U.S.
Consequences · two crises
Bay of Pigs (1961): CIA‑backed exile invasion fails
Failure pushes Castro closer to Moscow for protection
Cuban Missile Crisis (Oct. 1962): Soviet missiles — 13 days on the brink
Kennedy’s naval quarantine → Soviet withdrawal (a secret Turkey deal)
Your call — The world came closer to nuclear war in October 1962 than ever before or since. What made it end peacefully? ____________________________
