Concept Web
DOK 2 · Causation
When · Why A concept web surfaces the several drivers that converged on one outcome — American imperialism — and makes their relationships visible at a glance. Mapping multiple causes onto a single effect is core causal reasoning (TN Social Studies Practice SSP.05).
The hub names the outcome. On each spoke, add evidence for a driver that pushed the U.S. to look outward — then draw links between drivers that reinforced each other.
New Markets & Raw Materials
sell factory goods abroad · secure rubber, oil & sugar
Spreading Democratic & Moral Ideals
the stated goal — missionaries & a claimed ‘civilizing’ mission
Yellow Journalism & Public Opinion
Hearst & Pulitzer’s sensational stories stirred support
Naval Power
Mahan, The Influence of Sea Power upon History (1890) · bases & coaling stations
American
Imperialism
Make it work for every student UDL · MTSS

Scaffold

Word bank on the board: markets & raw materials · stated democratic/moral ideals · yellow journalism · naval power. Fill one spoke together with a linking stem: “This drove expansion because ___.”

Extend

Rank the four drivers — which mattered most in pushing the U.S. outward, and why? Defend it with one piece of evidence.

Show it your way

Students may write, say (record), or draw an icon in each bubble.
