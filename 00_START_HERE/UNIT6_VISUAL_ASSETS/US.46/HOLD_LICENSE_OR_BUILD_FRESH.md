## Exact blocker

All identified candidate maps for US.46 are hosted by the **USMA West Point
Digital History Center (DHC) atlas**:

- Europe: "German Expansion, 1936-39" — https://dhc.westpoint.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/6/2025/01/WWIIEurope02.jpg
- Asia/Pacific: "Major Japanese War Objectives and Planned Opening Attacks" — https://dhc.westpoint.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/6/2025/04/WWIIAsia04-01.jpg
- "Japanese Centrifugal Offensive December 1941" — https://dhc.westpoint.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/6/2025/04/WWIIAsia06-01.jpg
- "Imperial Powers, 1 September 1939" — https://dhc.westpoint.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/6/2025/04/WWIIAsia02-01.jpg

Source page: https://dhc.westpoint.edu/atlases/

These maps were created by USMA's Department of History (cartographers
Edward J. Krasnoborski, Frank Martini, Raymond Hrinko, Jeff Goldberg) and are
digitized versions of maps in atlases printed by the United States Defense
Printing Agency. **However, the atlas index page publishes no explicit
public-domain or open-license statement, and states verbatim:**

> "For permission to reprint these maps, please contact Mr. Jeff Goldberg at
> (845) 938-2264 or by email."

This is an explicit reprint-permission requirement from the rights holder /
publishing institution — not a blanket public-domain release. No image
binaries from this source have been downloaded or stored in this package.

## Required action — WRITTEN WEST POINT REPRINT PERMISSION REQUIRED

This standard specifically requires obtaining **written permission** from
USMA's Department of History before any of the above maps may be reprinted
in a commercial History Hack product:

1. Contact **Mr. Jeff Goldberg, USMA Department of History, (845) 938-2264**
   (or via the West Point DHC site's listed contact channel) and request
   written reprint/commercial-use permission for the specific map file(s)
   above, naming History Hack / TroopToTeacher Technologies LLC as the
   publisher.
2. Retain the written permission grant (email or letter) in the project's
   rights records.
3. Only after permission is confirmed in writing should the JPEG(s) be
   downloaded (atlas page states "Digitized maps are exported as 300 dpi
   JPEG files") and placed at `US.46_axis_expansion_map.jpg`.

## Safer path if permission is not obtained

**Build fresh**: produce an original Axis-expansion map (Europe & Asia,
1935–1941) on a public-domain/original basemap. The territorial facts
(dates, borders, occupied territories) are historical facts, not protected
expression, and may be re-plotted independently without licensing the West
Point cartography itself.
