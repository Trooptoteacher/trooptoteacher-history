- **Factual caveats — CAPTION ERROR RISK (REQUIRED NOTE):**
  - The sheet's own printed caption describing an accompanying photo records the opening session of the UNO as occurring at "Central Hall, London, 10 January, **1945**." **This is a documented error in the original 1946 source** — the opening session of the UN General Assembly actually took place on 10 January **1946**, not 1945. Do not treat the sheet's photo caption date as reliable; flag 1945/1946 as an error risk to students/instructors if this caption is visible or referenced.
