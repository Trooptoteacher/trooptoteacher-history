- **Factual caveats:**
  - Two aligned panels are used instead of a dual-axis chart, per design requirement.
  - Left panel distinguishes civilian LABOR FORCE (14.16M in 1940 → 19.03M in 1945) from EMPLOYED women (11.97M in 1940 → 18.61M in 1945) — these are different Census measures and must not be treated as interchangeable.
  - Right panel: female share of the civilian labor force = female civilian labor force ÷ total civilian labor force (both sexes), calculated from the same table: 25.4% (1940), 26.2% (1941), 28.6% (1942), 33.7% (1943), 35.1% (1944), 35.3% (1945).
  - Popular claims of "28%→36%" are not supported by this table; the verified range is approximately 25%→35%.
- **Alt text:** Two-panel chart: left panel shows women's civilian labor force (bars, 14.16M→19.03M) versus employed women (11.97M→18.61M) from 1940-1945; right panel shows the female share of the civilian labor force rising from 25.4% to 35.3% over the same period.
