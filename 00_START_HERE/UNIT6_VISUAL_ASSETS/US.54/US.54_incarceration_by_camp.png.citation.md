- **Factual caveats:**
  - "Resident population refers to population excluding evacuees on short-term and seasonal leave" (source table note).
  - 120,313 = total persons who came under WRA custody over the program's ENTIRE history (per Figure 1 in the source report); this is explicitly NOT the same as, nor a sum of, the ten peak resident populations shown on the chart bars (which sum to 112,581), because the peaks occurred on different dates and because the 120,313 total includes 757 institutionalized cases and 753 seasonal workers never assigned to a center.
  - Each bar is individually labeled with its peak date, underscoring that peaks are not simultaneous across camps.
- **Alt text:** Horizontal bar chart of the ten War Relocation Authority camps' peak resident populations (led by Tule Lake at 18,789 and Colorado River/Poston at 17,814), each labeled with its peak date, with a callout noting the bars sum to 112,581 — distinct from the 120,313 cumulative WRA custody total over the program's full history.
