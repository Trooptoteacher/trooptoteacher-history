- **Factual caveats:**
  - All 10 centers are labeled with their state abbreviations: Central Utah/Topaz (UT), Colorado River/Poston (AZ), Gila River (AZ), Granada/Amache (CO), Heart Mountain (WY), Jerome/Denson (AR), Manzanar (CA), Minidoka/Hunt (ID), Rohwer (AR), Tule Lake/Newell (CA).
  - **Symbols mark approximate post-office/county locations from WRA Table 5, NOT camp boundaries or exact site footprints** — this note is displayed directly on the map.
  - Marker positions are approximate placements derived from the named post office or county seat location for each center and are intended for classroom orientation only, not as authoritative GIS-grade site boundaries.
  - This map was built fresh because no official NPS map of WRA relocation-center locations could be located: the historical NPS URL now redirects to a program landing page, and the npshistory.com mirror carries no official-NPS statement and contains no map image.
- **Alt text:** U.S. map with square markers showing the approximate post-office/county locations of the ten War Relocation Authority incarceration centers for Japanese Americans during World War II, each labeled with its name and state abbreviation, sourced from the WRA's own 1946 statistical report.
