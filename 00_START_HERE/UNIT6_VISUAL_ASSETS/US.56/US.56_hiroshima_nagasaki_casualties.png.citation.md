- **Factual caveats:**
  - These are the 1946 MED report's own ESTIMATES, not exact counts. Report's verbatim uncertainty statement: "There has been great difficulty in estimating the total casualties in the Japanese cities as a result of the atomic bombing... the state of utter confusion immediately following the explosion, as well as the uncertainty regarding the actual population before the bombing, contribute to the difficulty of making estimates of casualties. The Japanese periodic censuses are not complete. Finally, the great fires that raged in each city totally consumed many bodies."
  - Time horizon is not stated numerically in Table A; treated as "as of the report's spring 1946 compilation."
  - Later estimates from other sources diverge widely and are NOT shown on this chart — only the 1946 MED figures are presented (Hiroshima: 66,000 dead / 69,000 injured; Nagasaki: 39,000 dead / 25,000 injured).
