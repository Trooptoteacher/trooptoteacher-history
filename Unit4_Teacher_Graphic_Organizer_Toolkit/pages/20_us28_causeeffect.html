When · Why Laying it out as push/pull → movement → impact makes visible how millions of decisions to leave the South reshaped both the region people left and the cities they built. Analyzing cause & effect is TN Social Studies Practice SSP.05.
Forces that pushed people out of the South and pulled them North sit on the left; four impacts flow out on the right. Arrows show the chain: causes → movement → effects.
Causes · push & pull factors
Push · Jim Crow segregation, disenfranchisement & racial violence
Push · sharecropping poverty & the boll weevil’s damage to cotton
Pull · WWI factory jobs at higher wages — ~$1,000/yr North vs. ~$300 South
The movement
THE GREAT MIGRATION, 1916–1970
~6 million African Americans move from the rural South to Northern & Midwestern cities — describe it in your words
Impacts · on North & South
Chicago, Detroit & New York grow; new Black neighborhoods & businesses form
New access to voting & political participation in the North
A flowering of culture — the Harlem Renaissance
The South loses working‑age population; new racial tensions rise in Northern cities
★ Tennessee Connection. Memphis and Nashville were key departure points for the Great Migration. Tennessee’s Black communities — especially in West Tennessee — shifted in large numbers to cities like Chicago, Detroit, and St. Louis.
